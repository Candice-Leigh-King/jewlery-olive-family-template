On 29 January 2024 Hind Rami Iyad Rajab who was a 5-year-old girl from Palestine, was killed by Israeli soldiers along side her 6 family members, and 2 paramedics coming to her rescue.

The Rajabs were fleeing Gaza City when their vehicle was shelled by the soldiers.

Little Hind was left stranded in the vehicle for hours on the phone, (the original phone call recordings are easy to find by searching them online) as paramedics from PRCS attempted to rescue her.

Unfortunatly, Hind and the rescue team were only later confirmed dead when their bodies were found on 10 February after an Israeli withdrawal.
