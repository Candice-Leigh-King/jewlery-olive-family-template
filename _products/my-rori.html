The meaning of Rori

Rori is the nickname of my daughter, Aurora — the diamond in my eye.

In Gaelic, “Rori” means red king and is rooted in the word for famous ruler or king. It feels fitting — because like every toddler, she truly rules the roost. "Rori" can also mean my light, which is also perfectly fitting.

A symbol of purity, unconditional love, and brilliance, this piece celebrates a light that never fades — the kind we find in the soul of motherhood, in purpose, and in the everlasting love that connects mother and child.
