Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: 14/10/2025

Thank you for shopping with Zaytun.

We hope you love your jewelry, but if something isn’t right, we’re here to help.


Refund & Replacement Eligibility

You can request a refund or replacement within 21 days of delivery if:

  • The item arrived damaged or defective
  • You received the wrong item or variant
  • An item is missing from your order
  • The item is significantly different from its description or images

    • To start a request, please email us at hizaytun@gmail.com with:

  • Your order number, and
  • Photo or video evidence showing the issue clearly

    • We’ll review your request and respond within 2–3 business days.


    When Refunds or Replacements Aren’t Available

    Refunds or replacements are not available if:

  • The item was delivered correctly and in good condition
  • The issue is due to buyer error (wrong size, color, or item)
  • You changed your mind after the order shipped
  • The delivery address provided was incorrect or incomplete
  • The order is delayed by customs, local postal issues, or uncontrollable logistics events

    • No Physical Returns

    Due to international shipping logistics, we do not accept returned items.
    All refund and replacement requests are processed electronically, based on the evidence provided. You will never need to ship a product back.

    Refund Processing

    Once approved, refunds are issued to your original payment method within 3–7 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.


    Contact Us

    If you have any questions about refunds, replacements, or delivery issues, please contact us at: 📧 hizaytun@gmail.com.

    We’re happy to help and aim to make every Zaytun experience a positive one.

    Shipping Policy

    Last updated: October 14, 2025

    Thank you for shopping with Zaytun! We proudly offer international shipping for all our jewelry.


    Shipping Destinations

    We ship to most countries worldwide.

    If your country isn’t available at checkout, please contact us at hizaytun@gmail.com.


    Processing Time

    Orders are processed within 1–5 business days after payment confirmation. Processing may take slightly longer during holidays or high-order periods.


    Estimated Delivery Times

  • United States: 7–15 business days
  • United Kingdom: 7–15 business days
  • Canada: 10–20 business days
  • Europe: 10–20 business days
  • Australia & New Zealand: 10–20 business days
  • Asia: 7–15 business days
  • Rest of World: 15–30 business days

    • Delivery times are estimates and may vary due to customs clearance or local postal delays.


    Shipping Costs

    Shipping is included for all standard deliveries. Optional express shipping upgrades may be available at checkout for some regions.

    Tracking Your Order

    You’ll receive a tracking number once your order ships. Tracking updates may take 3–5 days

    to appear. You can track your order via our Order Tracking page or the link in your confirmation email.

    Customs, Duties & Taxes

    International orders may be subject to import taxes, customs duties, or fees. These are not included in our prices and are the customer’s responsibility. Zaytun is not responsible for delays due to customs.

    Order Changes & Cancellations

    Contact Us
