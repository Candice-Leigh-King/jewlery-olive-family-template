ABOUT US

Zaytun was founded with a simple vision: to create jewelry that is both timeless and meaningful. Its story began in Durban, where the founder first worked in a jewelry house during her university years. There she discovered the art of sourcing, pairing, and curating pieces that weren’t just beautiful — they told a story. That experience became the foundation of Zaytun.

The name Zaytun means olive in Arabic — a symbol of peace, resilience, and endurance. Just as the olive tree has stood for centuries, Zaytun jewelry is created to last, designed with intention and crafted to carry meaning across generations.

Every collection reflects a commitment to understated elegance: pieces that are versatile enough for every day, yet refined enough to mark life’s milestones. Zaytun doesn’t follow fleeting trends. Instead, it creates jewelry that endures — in quality, in style, and in significance.

When you wear Zaytun, you don’t just wear gold.

You wear your part.
