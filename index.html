ABOUT US


A message from our founder, Candice King:

Zaytun began the way many sacred things do — with grief, hope, and the need to do something that truly mattered.

In the months following the genocide in Gaza, I found myself holding stories heavier than gold. Messages from mothers, children, strangers turned kin — people who had lost everything and still chose tenderness. I created The Olive Family, a WhatsApp and Facebook community where softness and resistance could exist together. A place for people who share the same values to connect — because being someone who cares deeply about humanity can feel isolating. These are spaces where talk turns into action. We fundraised, we mobilized, we showed up.

But with thousands of Palestinian families needing urgent and ongoing support, we knew we had to build something sustainable — something that could keep giving long after the headlines fade.

That’s how Zaytun was born.

So we can provide treatment to Palestinians with cancer.
So we can send emergency aid to families who have been displaced.
So we can keep showing up — again and again.

Named after the Arabic word for olive, Zaytun is a jewelry line rooted in kindness — designed to be worn with intention, meaning, and quiet strength. Each piece is a display of activism, a reminder that advocacy is breathtaking — and when worn boldly, it can change the world.

More than adornment, Zaytun funds The Olive Foundation, our nonprofit arm supporting displaced families, emergency response, and long-term dignity projects.

This brand carries everything I believe in: resistance is beautiful, kindness takes courage, and activism is refuge.

When you wear Zaytun, you don’t just wear gold.

You wear your part.
